# Complexity — Splay Tree

Single operation:
    O(h)
    h may be Theta(n)

Therefore worst-case single access:
    O(n)

Amortized sequence:
    O(log n) per search/insert/delete under classic analysis.

Traversal:
    Theta(n)

Storage:
    Theta(n)

No color/height metadata is stored.

Important:
Amortized O(log n) does not mean every operation is bounded by O(log n).
